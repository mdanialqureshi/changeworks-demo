# Changelog

## 0.9.0 (2026-11)

- `quill invoice create --at` dates an invoice in the customer's region.
- Emberlyn (KWD) region with banker's rounding.
- Credit notes and `quill invoice credit`.
- Payment allocation across invoices and instalment plans.

## 0.8.0 (2026-09)

- Coupons with priorities, validity windows and redemption limits.
- Corvane invoice-level tax rounding.
- JSON export.

## 0.7.0 (2026-07)

- First `quill` command line: customers, invoices, CSV export, aging.
